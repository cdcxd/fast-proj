BD-09 (Baidu): `BD09` and `bd09(options)`, a `Geographic` for WGS84 ↔ BD-09 (standalone or as a projection's `geographic`) that also converts GCJ-02 ↔ BD-09 directly (`toGcj02`, `fromGcj02` and their interleaved and point forms). Baidu's published offset over GCJ-02. `outsideChina` governs both offsets: in `'passthrough'` mode (default), points outside the China box are returned unchanged, as Baidu uses WGS84 outside mainland China, Hong Kong, Macau and Taiwan; `'transform'` applies both everywhere, as gcoord and coordtransform do. The forward is within 1.5e-14° of a 40-digit reference; the inverse takes two Newton steps and is within 1.5e-14° of the exact inverse, where gcoord's closed form is off by up to 2e-6° (0.2 m). A wasm context's `bd09()` runs the offset over its wasm GCJ-02.
