BD-09 (Baidu): `BD09` and `bd09(options)`, a `Geographic` for WGS84 ↔ BD-09 (standalone or as a projection's `geographic`) that also converts GCJ-02 ↔ BD-09 directly (`toGcj02`, `fromGcj02` and their interleaved and point forms). Baidu's published offset over GCJ-02, applied everywhere for compatibility with gcoord and coordtransform (Baidu's SDKs take WGS84 overseas, so keep WGS84 there); `outsideChina` governs the GCJ-02 step. The forward is within 1.5e-14° of a 40-digit reference; the inverse takes two Newton steps and is within 1.5e-14° of the exact inverse, where gcoord's closed form is off by up to 2e-6° (0.2 m). A wasm context's `bd09()` runs the offset over its wasm GCJ-02.
